# Home (reeveri.com /)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: founders, creators, and marketing leads deciding whether Reeveri gets attention. Action: Start a project. Proof: the site's own craft; work and content are clearly marked placeholders until the owner supplies real material. Constraints: brief pins near-black ground, off-white type, one sparing accent, big modern sans, no stock/fake claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Creative direction is selection. The page is a photographer's contact sheet: frames in rows, edge-printed numbers, and one red china-pencil mark choosing the keeper. It refuses the agency default of a showreel block, marquee strip, and card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Unlit film-base black #0b0b0a, print-white #efeee8, rebate gray #6d6d68, china-marker red #f2452d used only as hand marks (circles, strikes, arrows, crop brackets) and the primary action. Archivo alone: condensed caps for edge print metadata, heavy tight display, regular text. Frames are hairline-bordered rectangles with sprocket rows; no rounded cards, no shadows.

STORY: Visitor sees Reeveri pick what matters (the pencil circles "ignore"), believes they understand attention, scans services and work as a sheet under the loupe, and starts a project.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Headline "We make brands impossible to ignore." across the top two-thirds at ~9vw, red pencil circle drawing around "ignore" on load. Supporting line with Start a project (red, magnetic) and See our work in a right-hand column level with "ignore." (desktop), stacked below the headline on mobile; the short third line leaves that space. Full-bleed film strip of authored campaign frames advancing along the bottom, edge print above it, one frame circled as keeper.

FORM: Contact Sheet, grounded candidate 1 (Impeccable's pick, user-chosen); seed key ade88af7. Signature interaction: desktop loupe cursor that becomes a "View project" loupe over frames; pencil marks draw on scroll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
