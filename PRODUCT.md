# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router), React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, Lucide icons (only where an icon is genuinely needed).

## Users

Founders, creators, and marketing leads at startups and ambitious companies who are evaluating a creative growth partner. They arrive from social, referrals, or a direct link, and are deciding within minutes whether Reeveri understands attention, branding, content, and growth well enough to trust with their brand.

## Product Purpose

Reeveri is a creative marketing agency. The website's job is to make Reeveri feel like a high-end creative growth agency and turn qualified visitors into project enquiries ("Start a project"). Success is a visitor who believes Reeveri is sharp, creative, and results-minded, and starts a conversation.

## Positioning

Reeveri joins strategy, creative, content, technology, and distribution in one team: "Creative without strategy is decoration. Strategy without creativity is invisible." The site itself is the proof of craft.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse on phones (often from social) and desktops. They skim the hero, services, and work, then look for a way to start a project and for answers on process and cost.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: Social Media, Content & Creative, Performance Marketing, Website & Digital Experiences, Growth Strategy.
- Process: Understand, Strategize, Create, Launch, Learn.
- Pricing is not published; projects are scoped individually.
- Contact channel, email address, and social URLs are not yet provided (placeholders).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Reeveri; wordmark set as REEVERI.
- Personality: bold, creative, intelligent, modern, confident, slightly playful, human, results-oriented. Never corporate, formal, or old-school advertising.
- Voice: conversational, direct, a little witty. Avoid "We help businesses grow" template copy.
- Binding visual constraints from the owner: near-black ground with an off-white text system; one distinctive accent used sparingly; a modern premium sans-serif; large editorial typography; no stock photography, generic gradients, glassmorphism, heavy shadows, or card-grid SaaS look.
- Tagline: "Creative marketing for ambitious brands."

## Evidence on Hand

No real client names, logos, case studies, testimonials, results, awards, team members, or credentials exist yet. Work and content sections use clearly marked placeholders for the owner to replace. Never fabricate any of these.

## Product Principles

1. Show craft, don't claim it: the site's own art direction is the portfolio until real work arrives.
2. Strategy and creativity are always presented together.
3. Honesty over hype: no invented numbers, clients, or results.
4. Every section moves the visitor toward starting a project.
5. Human and confident, never corporate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: semantic HTML, keyboard access, visible focus, sufficient contrast, and full respect for prefers-reduced-motion. Custom cursor is desktop-only and never required for use.
